Who we are

Ifá is one of humanity's oldest libraries of knowledge — a vast body of verse, ethics, and cosmology, recognised by UNESCO as a masterpiece of intangible heritage. For generations it lived only in the memory of the Babaláwo. We are carrying it forward.

Orírùn Cultural Foundation exists to document this heritage faithfully, translate it widely, and place it in the hands of a new generation — without cost and without barriers.

We work in the space where tradition meets technology: honouring the elders who hold the knowledge, and using modern tools to make sure it is never lost.

The Orírùn app

Carry the wisdom with you.

Orírùn brings Ifá divination, Yorùbá numerology, and traditional herbal medicine into a calm, modern space — a place to reflect, learn, and heal.

  • Ifá divination by Odù, with orientation and guidance toward ẹbọ and adimu
  • Yorùbá numerology — a birth chart from your name and date, or a reading from a chosen number
  • Herbal medicinal formulas rooted in Yorùbá traditional medicine
  • Daily guidance drawn from the tradition
  • A learning corner to explore the meaning behind the signs
  • Available in 22 languages, free for everyone
